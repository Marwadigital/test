The project

Beachfront living, designed as a destination.

The Meriva Collection brings five residential buildings, signature beachfront residences and a dedicated hotel into one considered waterfront masterplan—with direct access to the beach.

It is Ellington’s first hospitality-led destination: a project designed not simply as a residential address, but as a place that remains active and relevant long after handover.

Why it stands out

A limited beachfront address in a city where true waterfront land is becoming rare.

Dubai Islands is one of the few large waterfront destinations still being developed at scale. Meriva is positioned directly at the shoreline, while remaining connected to the city’s key business, leisure and airport destinations.

  • Direct beach access
  • Five residential buildings and a dedicated hotel
  • Limited apartments and penthouses with sea views
  • Design-led developer: Ellington Properties
